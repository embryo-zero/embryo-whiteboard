# Embryo Whiteboard

Embryo Whiteboard is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Excalidraw](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw) (v0.18.0-469-gc0a0ca348, commit c0a0ca348). It does hand-drawn whiteboard and diagrams.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
